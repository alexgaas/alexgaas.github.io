Handmade with Love

Hi there! I'm Lisa Gaas, my creative journey started many years ago from making organic baby clothing. From fabric scraps I made my first toy named Mr. Scrappy Goose. He was so cute and loved by my kids that reminded me of my lovely bear from my childhood and inspired me to create a vintage teddy bear. Every creature is hand made with love in a highly professional manner!
