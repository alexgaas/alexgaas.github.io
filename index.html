Handmade with Love

Hi there! I'm Lisa Gaas, my creative journey started many years ago from making organic baby clothing. From fabric scraps I made my first toy named Mr. Scrappy Goose. He was so cute and loved by my kids that reminded me of my lovely bear from my childhood and inspired me to create a vintage teddy bear. Every creature is hand made with love in a highly professional manner!

Shipping & Policies

Shipping Information

Your purchase will be sent two days after funds are received by USPS inside of US. Tracking is provided where available. We take pride in our packaging to ensure your new friend arrives safely.

Rates: US (Ground Advantage) - $15-$35 depending on the weight and size.

If you are not completely happy with my creations, please notify me within 24 hours of receiving the bear. Send the bear in its original condition and packaging back for a refund.
