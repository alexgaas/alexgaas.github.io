Lisa Gaas, ScrappyGoose

 

Hi there! I'm Lisa Gaas, my creative jorney started many years ago from making organic baby clothing. From fabric scraps I made my first toy named Mr. Scrappy Goose. He was so cute and loved by my kids that reminded me of my lovely bear from my childhood and inspired me to create a vintage teddy bear. After passing many courses and master classes I started to create teddies using not only classic well-known patterns but also my own unique. I use the best high quality materials, natural soft stuffing and weight what makes a toy like realistic alive. Every creature is hand made with love in a highly professional manner!
